# Business Case

## Problem Statement

E-commerce organizations generate large volumes of operational data from customers, products, and transactions.

Raw data is often:

* Nested
* Inconsistent
* Difficult to analyze

Business teams require trusted datasets that support:

* Sales Reporting
* Inventory Analysis
* Customer Insights
* Executive Dashboards

This project demonstrates how a modern data platform transforms raw JSON data into business-ready datasets.

---

## Business Goals

### Sales Analytics

* Track revenue
* Identify top-performing products
* Analyze customer purchasing behavior

### Inventory Analytics

* Monitor stock levels
* Identify low inventory products
* Support replenishment decisions

### Customer Analytics

* Understand purchasing patterns
* Measure customer value
* Support segmentation

---

## Expected Outcomes

* Single source of truth
* Analytics-ready datasets
* Reduced query complexity
* Scalable architecture
* Cost-efficient storage
